# 🩺 NHANES Age Prediction | Summer Analytics 2026 Hackathon

A Machine Learning solution developed for the **Summer Analytics 2026 Hackathon**, organized by the **Consulting and Analytics Club, IIT Guwahati**.

The objective of this challenge was to predict whether an individual belongs to the **Senior (65+)** age group using health and nutrition data from a subset of the **National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey (NHANES)** conducted by the **CDC**.

---

## 📌 Problem Statement

The NHANES dataset contains health-related information collected through interviews, physical examinations, and laboratory tests.

The goal was to build a binary classification model capable of predicting:

* **0 → Adult (< 65 years)**
* **1 → Senior (≥ 65 years)**

The dataset consists of **6,287 samples** and **7 features** describing body measurements, lifestyle information, and laboratory results.
